## Payment and renewal

- Invoiced annually in advance. Late payment beyond 30 days, after written notice,
  suspends support per `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`.
- **Renewal (§307 BGB — attorney review required):** the term does not renew
  silently. The Provider gives written notice of the upcoming renewal **60 days**
  before term end; the customer may terminate with effect at term end on **30 days'
  notice**. Price holds for the committed term. Any price change at renewal is
  limited to the change in the German CPI (Verbraucherpreisindex) over the prior
  12 months, applied by a stated formula, and the customer may terminate instead
  of accepting it. Avoid "we may change prices at any time" wording — such a clause
  is generally invalid as an unreasonable standard-term disadvantage under §307 BGB.
