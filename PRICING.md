## What you are buying

- A **perpetual or term** license to use Aegis in production **without** the
  BSL-1.1 production-use restriction.
- The right to brand your deployment, provided attribution watermarks and copyright
  headers are retained (see `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md` §4).
- Updates and security fixes for the subscription term.

## What is NOT included

- Third-party vendored components (pdf.js, pdf-lib, tesseract.js, tessdata) remain
  under their own upstream licenses (Apache-2.0 / MIT) — see `docs/DEPENDENCY_AUDIT.md`.
- A third-party penetration test or ACR attestation — these are engagement-specific
  and quoted separately.
- German-language legal text localization of compliance templates beyond what ships
  in the product — available as a paid service.

## Payment and renewal

- Invoiced annually in advance. Late payment beyond 30 days, after written notice,
  suspends support and may terminate the license per `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md` §7.
- **Renewal (§307 BGB — attorney review required):** the term does not renew
  silently. The Provider gives written notice of the upcoming renewal **60 days**
  before term end; the customer may terminate with effect at term end on **30 days'
  notice**. Price holds for the committed term. Any price change at renewal is
  limited to the change in the German CPI (Verbraucherpreisindex) over the prior
  12 months, applied by a stated formula, and the customer may terminate instead
  of accepting it. Avoid "we may change prices at any time" wording — such a clause
  is generally invalid as an unreasonable standard-term disadvantage under §307 BGB.

small-organization production use) for evaluation. Production use beyond the free tier
