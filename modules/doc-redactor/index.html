Architecture & proof

How this engine works

  1. 01
    Load & rasterize

    pdf.js renders PDF pages to canvas at 150 dpi; images are decoded directly. Nothing is uploaded.

  2. 02
    In-browser OCR

    Tesseract (WebAssembly) runs in a local worker with bundled German/English models and returns word coordinates.

  3. 03
    Rules & review

    A regex catalogue (IBAN, VAT ID, tax ID, social-security number, contacts, addresses, case numbers …) plus a name heuristic. Every hit stays reviewable and removable.

  4. 04
    Destructive rebuild

    pdf-lib creates a new document: page = high-resolution raster + vector bars. Original text, fonts, annotations and XMP metadata are dropped.
