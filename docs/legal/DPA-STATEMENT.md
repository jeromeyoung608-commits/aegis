# Data processing — statement and short DPA

## 1. Default position

Aegis is self-hosted software. The Provider does not host, operate or access
Customer systems by default and therefore does not process personal data on
the Customer's behalf in the ordinary course. In that case no data processing
agreement is required.

## 2. When support access is granted

If the Customer grants the Provider remote or on-site access for support, the
Provider acts as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The following applies:

- **Subject matter and duration**: troubleshooting and support for the duration of the access granted.
- **Nature and purpose**: diagnosis, configuration, recovery; no other purpose.
- **Categories of data**: as determined by the Customer; the Provider does not request personal data and works with synthetic or redacted data where possible.
- **Sub-processors**: none without prior written authorisation.
- **Security measures**: access through the Customer's tooling, session logging, least privilege, deletion of local copies after the engagement.
- **Assistance**: the Provider assists with data-subject requests and breach notifications insofar as it is able.
- **Deletion**: local copies deleted within 10 business days after the engagement ends.
- **Audits**: one audit per year on 30 days' notice; reasonable security questionnaires answered.

## 3. Transfers

Where access from outside the EU is required, the parties use the EU Standard
Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and complete a transfer impact assessment before
access begins.
